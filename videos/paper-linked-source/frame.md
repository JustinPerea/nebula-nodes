# Paper-linked source demo

- Canvas: 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, 40 seconds.
- Brand: Slava black `#050506`, elevated charcoal glass, warm white ink. Orange
  is an interaction and emphasis signal; no orange background wash.
- Type: shipped Inter variable font for display/copy, shipped JetBrains Mono
  for labels. Main captions 70–99 px; explanation 27–31 px; proof labels 17 px.
- Structure: matching glass bookends, six real-product proof scenes. Browser
  captures sit beside large editorial copy; native Paper receives a wide
  actual-pixel crop. No recreated or substituted product controls.
- Motion: 0.5–0.65-second decelerating entrances, explicit state cuts, long
  reading holds. Inner copy and proof surfaces move; HyperFrames owns timed
  clip visibility. No crossfades between near-identical source states.
- Proof: exact logo before/after, explicit Refresh with stale earlier result,
  explicit saved-recipe rerun, both history records. Captured transition from
  Open in Paper control to desktop destination is an edited sequence.
- Honesty: downstream shots carry “Deterministic video fixture”. This is a
  source/export/replay demo and does not imply a paid generative-provider run.
- Audio: intentionally silent, captioned. All dependencies are local.
- Publication plates: native Paper PNGs are lossless crops of the decoded real
  captures, removing only the top 80 pixels with unrelated project tab titles.
  Original JPEG captures stay local and ignored. Framing compensates for the
  removed strip so the visible logo and Paper context retain their placement.

The composition follows the existing `codex-chat-announcement` structure and
the `nebula-nodes-visuals` motion grammar, updated for current clip ownership
and root sizing rules.
